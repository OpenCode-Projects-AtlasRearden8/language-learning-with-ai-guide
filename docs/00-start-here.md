# Start Here

This is a manual for learning a language with AI as your **patient practice
partner** — not your teacher, not your replacement, not a vending machine for
answers. Purely a lab partner who never gets tired, never laughs at your
mistakes, and is free to talk to at 6 a.m. before your shift.

Written the way Dylan Asdale writes about AI: plain English, step by step,
never condescending. Like a friend explaining it over coffee.

## Who this is for

Anyone learning a language around a real life — work, nursing shifts, kids,
commutes. The core promise of this guide is the title of chapter 1: fifteen
minutes a day beats the heroic ninety-minute plan you keep abandoning.

## What you need

- **A language you want to learn** (or refresh). Any language works.
- **An AI chat you can talk to.** A local model on your own computer (free,
  private, works offline) is ideal — chapter 2 shows how — but a free hosted
  chat works too.
- **A plain text folder.** That's your school. Chapters 4 and 5 show you how
  a folder full of ordinary `.csv` and `.md` files replaces fancy apps.

## A quick word on the sister guide

This guide pairs with **Study Smarter with AI** (`study-smarter-ai-guide`),
which covers the study skills that apply to *any* subject: turning notes into
quizzes, the explain-it-back technique, catching hallucinations, and the
theory behind spaced review. This guide does not repeat those — it builds on
them and often points you there. If a chapter here says "the sister guide
explains why this schedule works," that's what it means.

## The five-finger summary

1. **Short and daily beats long and someday.** Fifteen minutes, every day.
2. **AI is your conversation partner, not your oracle.** Correct *it* — you
   learn more by finding its mistakes than by accepting its answers.
3. **Everything lives in plain files.** If an app dies, your vocabulary
   doesn't.
4. **Trust real sources for rules, use AI for practice.** Chapter 5 is
   non-negotiable.
5. **Grace over guilt.** Skipping a week is a pause, not a resignation.
