# Weekly Plan Cards

Print or copy one card per week into your notes. Each card is twelve-ish
checkboxes and nothing more: the drill row, one set of boxes a day. A
checked box is the smallest possible public promise to yourself, and
the cards are built so even a bad week leaves a trail of checked boxes
instead of a wall of guilt.

How to choose: **Card 1 for weeks 1–4** (chapter 1's starter plan). After
that, pick the card that matches what you most need this particular
week. When stuck or plateaued, chapter 8 maps plateau weeks to cards.

---

## Card 1 — Word Week (weeks 1–4, and any return-after-a-break week)

**Focus: build or rebuild the daily habit. Lightest possible lift.**

```
Week of: ________   Language: ________   Level: ________

Mon  ☐ warm up ☐ drill (same one all week) ☐ input ☐ add 2 words ☐ my one sentence
Tue  ☐ drill  ☐ add 2 words ☐ one sentence
Wed  ☐ drill  ☐ add 2 words ☐ one sentence
Thu  ☐ drill  ☐ add 2 words ☐ one sentence
Fri  ☐ drill  ☐ add 2 words ☐ one sentence
Sat  ☐ input day — one song or one film scene, enjoy it (20–45 min)
Sun  ☐ rest or 2-minute vocab tap

One line for the week: what did I actually catch myself using? __________
```

**The rule this week:** never miss twice in a row. Bad Tuesday? Do the
Wednesday 2-minute version, not the triple session.

---

## Card 2 — Sentence Week

**Focus: full sentences only. The one-line day diary begins.**

```
Week of: ________   Language: ________   Level: ________

Mon  ☐ drill, every answer must be a full sentence ☐ one sentence
Tue  ☐ drill ☐ steal 3 sentence frames into my phrase list ☐ one sentence
Wed  ☐ drill ☐ tomorrow's diary line, written tonight ☐ one sentence
Thu  ☐ drill ☐ read phrase list aloud twice ☐ one sentence
Fri  ☐ drill ☐ diary line about today ☐ one sentence
Sat  ☐ input day + copy 1 line I liked, annotated ☐ one sentence
Sun  ☐ rest or 2-minute vocab tap

One line for the week: one sentence frame I now own: ______________
```

**The rule this week:** "full sentence" means you said it, not
genre-perfect. Grammar polish is next week's problem.

---

## Card 3 — Listening Week

**Focus: the ear. Partner speaks first; you follow.**

```
Week of: ________   Language: ________   Level: ________

Mon  ☐ drill, partner-led: they speak, I answer ☐ one sentence
Tue  ☐ one song, listen twice, note 3 words I caught ☐ one sentence
Wed  ☐ drill at speed: follow, don't translate ☐ one sentence
Thu  ☐ re-listen Tuesday's song — more words should land ☐ one sentence
Fri  ☐ drill, eyes off: pauses closed, ears open ☐ one sentence
Sat  ☐ 20–30 min one film/TV scene, target-language subtitles ☐ one sentence
Sun  ☐ rest or 2-minute vocab tap

One line for the week: a voice that scared me a month ago and doesn't now: _____
```

**The rule this week:** comprehension before production. If you
understood more than you could say, the week worked.

---

## Card 4 — Rescue Week (bad-life week: shifts, sickness, moving boxes)

**Focus: protect the identity, not the progress. Minimum viable
sessions (chapter 8) only.**

```
Week of: ________   Language: ________   Level: ________

Mon  ☐ vocab due-list only
Tue  ☐ one sentence out loud
Wed  ☐ two minutes of a song in the language
Thu  ☐ vocab due-list only
Fri  ☐ one sentence out loud
Sat  ☐ whatever small thing sounds nice ☑ that's allowed
Sun  ☐ rest

One line for the week: I stayed in the language this week. Yes ☐ / I mean it ☐
```

**The rule for this card:** no guilt table, no review of the week. The
card IS the week. Four of seven boxes checked = maintenance succeeded.

---

## Card 5 — My Card (weeks 5+, write your own)

Copy Card 1's skeleton, keep the seven-day shape, and change exactly
one thing per week: a new drill (chapter 3), a new medium (chapter 6),
a travel countdown day (chapter 7), or a plateau protocol week
(chapter 8). One change per card — a card with three new things is a
plan for a different person, not for you.

**Design questions for your own card, one per week:**

- What did this week's sessions make easy that was hard a month ago?
- What do I still do in English that I could try in the language?
- What single phrase, if automatic, would unlock the most conversation?

After the week, the card gets one completed line and one honest line —
then it goes in the folder. Old cards are your progress record, and
they're worth more than any app counter.
